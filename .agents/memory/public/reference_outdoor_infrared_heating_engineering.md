**Archived:** September 2026  
**Context:** Specification, brand tiering, electrical calculations (NEC 424/300), and structural fence mounting for residential outdoor infrared radiant heating.

## 1. High-Output Electric IR Heating (4,500W / 240V)

### Electrical Calculations & NEC Sizing Rules
- **Formula:** $\text{Amps} = \frac{4500\text{W}}{240\text{V}} = 18.75\text{A}$
- **NEC Continuous Load Sizing (Article 424):** Electric space heating is classified as a continuous load requiring a $125\%$ branch circuit multiplier:
  $$18.75\text{A} \times 1.25 = \mathbf{23.44\text{A}}$$
- **Required Breaker:** Dedicated **30A 240V double-pole breaker** (a 20A 240V breaker will nuisance trip under sustained operation).
- **Conductor Sizing:** Minimum **10 AWG copper** (10/2 with ground NM-B Romex indoors; 10 AWG THHN/THWN-2 in conduit outdoors).
- **GFCI Requirement:** Outdoor 240V heating circuits require a **2-pole 30A GFCI breaker** (NEC 210.8(F)).
- **Conduit Requirements (NEC 300.5):** Schedule 40 PVC buried minimum 18" deep underground; transitions to Schedule 80 PVC above ground on posts/walls where subject to physical damage. Must terminate into a NEMA 3R/4 weatherproof junction box with liquid-tight flexible conduit (LFMC).

## 3. Structural & Thermal Clearance for Wooden Fence Mounting

1. **Structural Load:** A 61" 4500W heater weighs 22–26 lbs and acts as a wind sail. **Never mount to fence pickets.** Must lag-bolt directly into structural 4x4 or 6x6 fence posts using 3/8" × 3" stainless lag screws.
2. **Thermal Clearances:** Elements operate at 1,200°F–1,400°F; aluminum back casing reaches 160°F–220°F. Requires minimum 6" bracket standoff from wood and a non-combustible thermal barrier (1/4" HardieBacker or stainless heat shield plate) on the post.
3. **Geometry & Beam Angle:** At 8 ft height angled down 30°–45°, the radiant heat footprint lands 5 to 8 feet out from the fence line.

## 4. Square Seating Area Thermal Dynamics & Alternatives

- **Linear Wall/Fence Heaters on Square Seating:** Single-direction heat creates uneven exposure (hot back / cold chest).
- **Center Radiant Coffee Tables (Electric 360° / Under-Table):** 120V 1500W footwell heating warms extremities directly with zero glare. Great for 50°F–65°F evenings.
- **Center Propane Fire Pit Coffee Tables:** 40,000–55,000 BTU 360-degree radiant heat, zero electrical trenching, drinks ledge, acts as natural gathering centerpiece.
