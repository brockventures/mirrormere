Completion alerts generated by `detached_runner.py` were silently trapped in `data/outbox/pending.jsonl` due to a missing delivery daemon trigger rather than actively dispatching. Systems relying on asynchronous background completion alerts must ensure active outbox flushing rather than relying on uncoupled daemon polling.
