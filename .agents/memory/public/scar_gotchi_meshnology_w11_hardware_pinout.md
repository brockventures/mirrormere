Identified the Gotchi hardware board as a Meshnology W11-MB-V0.2 (ESP32-S3 Mini Module) with 8 edge holes per side, resolving pin mismatch against standard Seeed XIAO pinouts to prevent miswiring into the camera parallel data bus. Established mounting mechanicals by clipping the camera spring directly onto the 17.8 mm Sense PCB edges between L2 and D1 for a captive board-and-spring assembly.
