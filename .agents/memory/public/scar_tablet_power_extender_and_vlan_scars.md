The wall tablet crash/shutdown issues were traced to a faulty Amazon barrel cable extender causing power loss despite AC reporting, alongside a network misconfiguration where SciWiFi-IoT's VLAN isolation blocked LAN access to Brock Hearth. The tablet must remain locked to primary SciWiFi with SciWiFi-IoT purged from saved networks.
