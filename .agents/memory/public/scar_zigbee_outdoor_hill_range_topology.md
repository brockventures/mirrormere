Ryan's outdoor Zigbee plug drops with LQI 0 because it lacks intermediate repeaters toward the top of the hill, isolated from the 4 indoor routers. Bridging requires placing an indoor Zigbee repeater in the back of the house/kitchen (such as a ThirdReality smart plug or high-gain USB antenna router in a back window) to link indoor leak sensors and push range up the hill.
