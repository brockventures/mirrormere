The top-floor Sonoff ZBDongle-E USB repeater facing the backyard is active and functioning properly, so it should remain indoors rather than being moved outside. Ground-level soil sensors attenuate heavily due to damp soil and flush mounting, requiring line-of-sight to the outdoor smart plug or inspection of the battery cap rather than indoor repeater relocation.
